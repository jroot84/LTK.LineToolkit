Privacy Policy

Line Toolkit (LTK) · Last updated August 2026

Line Toolkit is a personal field tool built to run entirely in your browser. This policy explains, plainly, what happens to your data.

What's stored, and where

Everything you enter — cabinet and panel records, port labels and notes, MAC vendor entries you save, IPDB registry entries, and job checklists with photos — is saved using your browser's local storage, on your device only. None of it is sent to any server operated by this app. There is no account, no sign-in, and no backend database.

Clearing your browser's site data, or uninstalling the app if installed as a PWA, deletes this data permanently. There is currently no cloud backup or sync.

Requests this app makes to outside services

A few tools in the app need to reach the open internet to work. When you use them, your device connects directly to these third parties — this app itself does not see or log that traffic:

  • MAC / OUI Lookup — runs entirely from a bundled local list plus anything you've saved yourself. No network request is made.
  • Public IP / ASN lookup — when you tap "Check public IP" or look up an address, your device queries ipapi.co, which will see the IP address being queried (including your own device's public IP, if you check your own connection). See their own privacy terms for how they handle that.
  • Speed test — downloads and uploads test data directly to Cloudflare's public network-test endpoints (speed.cloudflare.com) to measure throughput. No test data is retained by this app.
  • Location tagging — if you tag a cabinet or job location, your device's Geolocation API is used with your explicit tap, and the coordinates are saved only in your local storage, not transmitted anywhere by this app.
  • Fonts — type is loaded from Google Fonts on first load, which involves a request to Google's font servers.

Analytics and tracking

This app does not use analytics, ad tracking, or third-party trackers of any kind.
